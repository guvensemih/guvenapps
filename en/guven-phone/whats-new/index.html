What's new in Güven Phone

This page lists what is new in each Güven Phone version released to everyone on Google Play. The text comes from the Release notes screen in the app and the release notes on Google Play.

Version 2.0.22

Available to everyone on Google Play since 23 October 2026.

A lot has arrived since 2.0.12. Below you will find what each feature does and where it is; Help Guide in Settings explains them in detail.

In short

  • Contacts hold several numbers, email, company and birthday; import or export VCF.
  • Add call opens a searchable contact list; Bluetooth devices are detected reliably.
  • Call details open full screen, with weekly, monthly and yearly statistics.
  • Optional: turn over to silence, raise to your ear or answer automatically.
  • Assign contacts to keys 2–9 and hold a key to call; key 1 calls voicemail.
  • Güven ringtones, caller announcement speed and volume, number reading style.

Adding and editing contacts

A contact can have several phone numbers, each with its own type: Mobile, Home, Work, Other or your own label. There are also email, company, job title and birthday fields. Start with the Add new contact button on the Contacts tab. A number you type on the Keypad tab can be saved to a new or an existing contact with the Add to contacts button.

How to use

Contact actions: transfer, groups and SIM phonebook

The Contact actions menu at the top of the Contacts tab has four options. Import contacts and Export contacts work with VCF files; before an import the file is checked and contacts you already have are set apart. Contact groups lets you create groups and add members. SIM phonebook shows the entries on your SIM card and copies them to your contacts.

How to use

Contact details

Contact details now open full screen. From there you can call, send a message, edit the contact, add it to favorites, choose the default number, set a custom ringtone and block the number. Tapping a contact in the Contacts list (double-tapping with a screen reader) calls it; the details and other actions are in your screen reader’s actions menu. If you turn off Tap to Call in the Accessibility section of Settings, tapping a contact opens its details.

How to use

During a call

The Add call button on the call screen opens a searchable contact list with letter headings; you can join two calls with Merge. If a Bluetooth headset or watch is connected, the Bluetooth button sends the sound to it; with several devices a list opens to choose from. For this, grant the Bluetooth call devices permission once in the Permissions and Privacy section of Settings. With Automatic Speaker on, the sound stays in the earpiece while the phone is at your ear and moves to the speaker when you take it away.

How to use

Incoming calls: turn over, raise and auto answer

Three optional settings sit at the end of the Accessibility section of Settings. Turn Over to Silence: turning a ringing phone face down silences the ringer and the caller announcement. Raise to Answer: raising the phone to your ear silences the ringer, and the call is answered when the sensor detects your ear. Auto Answer: the call is answered by itself after the wait you choose, if you like only with a headset connected or only for your saved contacts. A call whose ringer you silenced is never answered by itself.

How to use

Recent Calls and statistics

Opening a call entry shows its details full screen, with your whole call history with that number and actions such as Call Back, Send message and Add to Contacts. From the Options menu at the top of Recent Calls you can open Call statistics: total calls, talk time and the people you talk to most, per week, month and year; you can copy or share the report.

How to use

Speed dial and voicemail

Open Speed dial in Settings and assign a contact to a key from 2 to 9. On the Keypad tab, with the number field empty, long-press that key to call the contact; with a screen reader, find the key, double-tap and keep your finger down on the second tap. Long-pressing 1 calls voicemail; you can enter the voicemail number for each SIM on the same screen, under key 1.

How to use

Ringtones

The Ringtone section of Settings has eight Güven Phone ringtones; listen to each with Preview and make one your phone’s ringtone. On the first choice Android asks for permission to modify system settings; the Open permission screen button takes you there. To give one contact its own ringtone, use Custom ringtone in the contact’s details.

How to use

Caller announcement and number reading

In the Accessibility section of Settings, with Announce Caller on, you choose how many times the caller is announced, the speed, the volume and which sound it uses: Accessibility volume, Media volume or Ringtone volume. Phone number reading style sets how numbers are read: Accessible format (recommended), Digit by digit or Two-digit groups.

How to use

Favorites

Favorites are now shared with Android’s favorites, so favorites added in other apps appear here too. Change their order with Move up and Move down in your screen reader’s actions menu.

How to use

Reporting a problem

If something goes wrong, open Report a problem at the top of Settings. Describe the problem briefly if you like, then tap Send (with a screen reader, double-tap). The report contains no names, phone numbers or messages.

How to use

Questions and feedback

The Help Guide explains each feature step by step; the same guide is in the app's Settings. For questions and suggestions, see the community and feedback section.
