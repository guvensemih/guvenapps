Accessible Android apps

Güven Apps is an independent app brand developing accessible and easy-to-use Android applications.

Logo description

The Güven Apps logo features a stylized letter G in dark navy and teal, shaped like a rounded shield and speech bubble. Six dots inside the symbol represent a Braille cell and accessibility.

Güven Phone

From inside the app

In Güven Phone, open Report a problem at the top of Settings. Describe the problem briefly if you like.

Send lets you send the report with an app you choose, such as email. If you choose email, address it to guvenapps80@gmail.com. Save to file saves the report on the phone.

The report contains app and device information and a short log of recent events. It contains no names, phone numbers or messages.
